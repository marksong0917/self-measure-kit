Measure your space in about 5 minutes

Answer a few questions, add photos, and you get a clean summary the shop can quote from — no site visit needed for a first price.

1. What are we measuring?

Please pick a space to continue.

2. Room size

Measure wall to wall, in centimetres (cm). Not sure? A tape measure and 2 minutes is all it takes — round to the nearest cm.

Working in feet and inches? 1 ft = 30.48 cm and 1 in = 2.54 cm — so an 8 ft ceiling is 244 cm, a 10 ft wall is 305 cm.

Length Width A B C D

3. Wall by wall

Tick the walls where you want cabinets or storage, then count the doors, windows and obstacles on each one. Wall lengths come from your room size: A and C = length, B and D = width.

Pick at least one wall with cabinets or storage — the cabinet run and price range come from these walls.

4. Photos of the space

One photo per wall helps the shop more than measurements alone. Stand in the corners and shoot wide.

In this demo photos stay on your device — they are only previewed here and are never uploaded. In the live version they attach to your summary for the shop.
